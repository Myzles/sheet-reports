How do I stop a report?

Open any spreadsheet, then Extensions, Scheduled Sheet Reports, Manage schedules. Every schedule you own is listed, including ones in other files. Press Pause or Delete; it takes effect immediately. To stop everything, use Extensions, Scheduled Sheet Reports, Pause all my schedules. Uninstalling the add-on also stops all of them.

A report didn't arrive

  • Open Manage schedules and look at the schedule's status and History. It says what happened at each run.
  • Reports go out during the hour you chose, not on the minute. Times use the spreadsheet's time zone (File, Settings).
  • Check the recipient's spam folder, especially for the first report.
  • If the sidebar says Google hasn't checked the file for a while, press "Repair the timer".
  • Google limits how many people you can email per day: about 100 for free Gmail accounts and 1,500 for Workspace. Each recipient counts. A report held by the limit goes out when it resets, the same day.

The PDF looks wrong

The PDF follows the sheet's own column widths and formatting. Narrow the columns or pick a smaller range, then press "Send me a test" to compare. Hidden rows and columns are left out of the Excel, CSV and email table.

Which formats can I send?

PDF, Excel (.xlsx), CSV and a table in the email body, in any combination. For the whole file, Excel is Google's full export with every tab, and CSV sends one file per tab.

Can I put numbers from the sheet in the subject?

Yes. Use {{B2}} for a cell on the same tab or {{Summary!B2}} for another tab. Also {{date}}, {{time}}, {{day}}, {{month}}, {{file}} and {{tab}}.
