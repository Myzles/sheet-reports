Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Scheduled Sheet Reports is a Google Sheets add-on made by Myzles ("we"). This policy explains what the add-on can access, what it does with it, and what it never does.

What the add-on accesses, and why

  • The spreadsheet you use it in (spreadsheets.currentonly): to read the range, tab or file you chose to send. It cannot open your other spreadsheets.
  • Files you use with the add-on (drive.file): so Google can export that spreadsheet as a PDF or Excel file. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • Sending email as you (script.send_mail): to send the reports you set up, to the addresses you typed, from your own Google account. The add-on cannot read your email.
  • Running when you are not present (script.scriptapp): one hourly timer per spreadsheet, so scheduled reports go out on time.
  • Your email address (userinfo.email): to send you test reports and a notice if a report fails.
  • Showing its sidebar (script.container.ui) and connecting to an outside service (script.external_request): used to reach Google's own export service and to check a license key with our payment provider, Polar.

How your data is used

Everything runs in Google Apps Script, on Google's servers, under your Google account. Spreadsheet contents are read only to build the reports you scheduled, and are sent only to the recipients you entered. We never receive, store, sell or share your spreadsheet data or your recipients' addresses.

Your schedules (what to send, to whom and when) and a short send history are saved in your Google account's add-on storage, which only the add-on running as you can read. If you enter a license key, it is saved there too and sent to Polar (polar.sh) to confirm it is valid. No other information goes with it.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Polar Software Inc. as merchant of record. Polar collects your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy (polar.sh/legal/privacy). We receive your email address and order details from Polar so we can provide support.

Google user data

Scheduled Sheet Reports' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and no person reads it.

Cookies and tracking

The add-on uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and adds no tracking to the emails it sends. This website uses none either.

Removing your data

Delete your schedules in the sidebar, or uninstall the add-on (Extensions, Add-ons, Manage add-ons) to remove its access; its timers stop with it. To have us delete your order records, email us at the address below.
