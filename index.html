Scheduled Sheet Reports

Email your Google Sheets™ reports on a schedule. Stop them with one click.

Pick a range, a tab or the whole file. Choose PDF, Excel, CSV or a table right in the email. Pick the day and hour. It goes out from your own Gmail™, and you can always see when it last ran.

Free for one schedule. No account to create.

Every schedule in one list

See every report you have set up, in every spreadsheet, from any spreadsheet. Pause or delete one and it stops right away. "Pause all" stops everything at once.

You can tell it's working

Each schedule shows when it last ran, what it sent, when it runs next and a short history. If Google hasn't checked a file for a while, the sidebar says so and fixes it in one click.

When something breaks, you hear about it

Google hiccups are retried on their own. If a report still can't go out (a deleted tab, a typo in an address), you get one email saying what happened and how to fix it. Not one a day.

The format your team wants

A PDF of exactly the range or tab you picked. Excel or CSV of exactly the range you picked. Or a formatted table in the email body, colors and bold included.

Send only when it matters

Hold a report back when the range is empty, when nothing changed since last time, or until a cell says TRUE. Put live numbers in the subject with {{Summary!B2}}.

Your data stays in Google

Runs on Google's servers under your account and your Gmail™ sending limit. Nothing is copied to our servers, because we don't have any.

How it works

  1. Open your spreadsheet, then Extensions, Scheduled Sheet Reports, New schedule.
  2. Choose what to send and the format, add the email addresses, pick when.
  3. Press "Send me a test" to see exactly what arrives. Then Save.

Reports go out during the hour you choose (Google runs add-on timers once an hour), in your spreadsheet's time zone. Daylight saving changes never move them.
